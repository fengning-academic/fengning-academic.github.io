About

I am a doctoral student at the School of Civil Engineering, Central South University. My research focuses on the numerical simulation of the mechanical behavior of railway and urban rail transit structures, track vibration and noise reduction, experimental studies, and damage identification and monitoring.

Please feel free to email me to discuss potential collaboration. fengning0919@163.com
